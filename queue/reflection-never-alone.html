Turning Points · 10

Awards are never won alone

There's one name missing from every trophy we've ever lifted.

Everyone's.

In 2017, before I'd even joined as director, my partner David was named Dispensing Optician of the Year at the Hakim Group Awards. I remember feeling that I'd walked into something special.

In 2019, Clamp was named Practice of the Year, and Audiology Practice of the Year.

In 2020, we were named Eye Care Specialists of the Year at the Cambridgeshire Prestige Awards.

And in 2025, Best Eyewear Store in Cambridge.

It would be easy to write those as a list of achievements. But that's not how they feel.

Behind every one is a receptionist who made a nervous patient smile. A colleague who stayed late. A dispenser who took twenty extra minutes to get a frame just right. A team who covered for each other on difficult days.

And behind all of us, the patients who trusted us with something precious: their sight.

I've learned that leadership isn't standing at the front. It's making sure everyone knows the trophy is theirs too.

At this time of year, I find myself simply grateful.

For the people who carry each other. For the patients who come back. For the quiet grace that has carried me through years I didn't expect to survive.

Who deserves a share of your last success?
