Being Seen · Reflection 01

For most of my childhood, I wanted to disappear

At four years old, white patches began to appear around my eyes and across my body. Vitiligo. I was the only one in my family it found, and I felt as though everyone was staring.

So I learned to go quiet. I did my talking on paper, where I felt safe and sharp, and let the playground get on without me.

Today, I spend my working days looking into people's eyes. And somewhere along the way I learned something I wish I could tell that boy: looking and seeing are not the same thing.

Looking is what eyes do. Seeing is what happens when someone feels noticed, understood and valued, exactly as they are.

That's what I'll be writing about here, week by week: the art of seeing, and being seen, in the consulting room and in life.
